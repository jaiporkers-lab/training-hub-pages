# Roadmap

Training Hub focuses on local analysis and preserving original recordings. Each feature should distinguish measurements from estimates and handle gaps in the source data.

## Available

- FIT import, multisport grouping, duplicate protection and resumable Garmin Personal API sync.
- A customisable activity feed and Home, weekly trends, health comparisons, and a local coaching prompt builder.
- Time- and distance-based comparison, matched race legs, lap and zone analysis, pool lengths, outdoor heatmap and route inspection.
- Backup and restore with validation, CSV export, and a guided welcome screen.

## Under consideration

- Training calendar, planned workouts and race milestones.
- Cycling power-duration curves, repeated-climb analysis, running consistency and heart-rate drift.
- Gear mileage and service reminders.
- Route matching, metric-coloured routes and exportable race reports.

See [feature ideas](FEATURE-IDEAS.md) for details and the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) for shipped changes.
