03 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

Make room for
what comes next.

Plan, review and organise your training. Recovery trends sit alongside volume, so you can explore your own recorded patterns.

05

A calendar that moves with you.

Plan workouts and race dates beside completed recordings. Drag a plan to another day. Everything stays local and is included in library backups. Watch-calendar publishing is unavailable through the Personal API connection.

06

Targets you choose.

Set weekly time and distance goals, track a distance milestone and scan your library for running or cycling best efforts.

07

Recovery in context.

Align daily training with saved sleep, HRV, resting HR and other health metrics. Explore rolling means and same-day or next-day associations, with recorded-day counts.

Descriptive patterns, without claims of causation.

08

Your Home, your way.

Start with running, cycling, swimming, triathlon or recovery presets. Pick the cards and sport focus you want, then save your own layout.

04 / YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR MAC

Personal data.
Personal control.

GarHub processes your activities locally. The public project contains the app source and docs, without your keys, health records or training library.

Read the security model ↗
01

Keys in macOS Keychain.

The optional Garmin connection uses your Personal API key. Enter it in the welcome screen or Settings. It is kept out of library backups and logs.

02

Local calculations. No AI service.

Charts and analysis run on your Mac. The optional prompt builder prepares text locally, which you can review and copy yourself.

03

Network use you can see.

Garmin sync contacts Garmin. Optional Apple Maps basemaps receive the viewed area. This website has no analytics, accounts or training-data uploads.

04

Open about the limits.

This is a vibe-coded independent project with automated regression checks, not an independently audited or Garmin-certified app. Source, build instructions and security reporting are public.

  1. Open the disk image and drag GarHub into Applications.

The download is ad-hoc signed and is not Apple-notarised. macOS may ask you to allow it in Privacy & Security. Review the build and installation notes before installing.
